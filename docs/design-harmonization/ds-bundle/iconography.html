Foundations

Iconography & marks

Icons come from @radix-ui/react-icons at 15×15, sized by Tailwind rather than by prop. Two hand-drawn 24×24 stroke icons live in icons.tsx for concepts Radix has no glyph for. Three marks are brand assets: the moku pona logo, the lockup, and the Google G.

app/components/icons.tsx · app/components/logo.tsx · app/components/brand-lockup.tsx · app/features/auth/components/google-button.tsx

Sizes — three, and only three

size-3 inside badges
size-4 the default — buttons, facts, fields
size-5 nav, notices, mobile menu
size-6 / size-7 empty-state and auth-status medallions

Icons inherit currentColor. When an icon is decorative beside text it is dimmed to text-foreground/50; when it carries the meaning (event date, accordion chevron, mobile menu chevron) it takes text-primary.
