Signup form builder

The largest composite in the app: the admin editor for a dinner's signup questions. Every question is a collapsible RowCard; three tints classify a row at a glance — plain for an ordinary question, primary for a pinned identity field that cannot be removed, sky for the friends group. It degrades without JavaScript: a <noscript> rule forces every row body open, and reorder/insert/remove are Conform intent buttons.

RowCard rounded-lg border — 8px, not the card 16px, because rows are controls in a list. Tints: border-primary/35 bg-primary/10 pinned identity · border-sky-300/35 bg-sky-300/10 friends group · none for the rest. A row with nested errors is forced open regardless of its collapse state.

RowHeader and ActionRow

RowHeader — the title area is itself a CollapsibleTrigger (min-w-0 flex-1 cursor-pointer text-left) with a truncating text-base font-semibold tracking-tight title over a text-xs meta line. Then three size="icon" ghost buttons: move up, move down, toggle — the toggle borrows buttonVariants through cn() because it must render as a trigger, not a button.

ActionRow — negative margins (-mx-4 -mb-4) pull it to the row body's edges so its border-t spans the full card, then px-4 pt-3 pb-4 restores the padding. Actions are right-aligned and wrap.

Link options — a radio card

flex cursor-pointer gap-2 rounded-lg border p-3; checked takes the primary tint, unchecked hovers to bg-foreground/5. The native radio keeps accent-primary rather than being replaced — the one place a browser control is left as-is.

Builder-level actions

Both outline, stacked on mobile (flex-col sm:flex-row). "Reset to default" confirms through window.confirm, with a harsher message once the dinner has signups and removing a field would orphan answers.
