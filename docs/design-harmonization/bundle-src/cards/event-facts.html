The four facts

EventDateHeading saturday, 14 september · 19:00
EventLocationFact langstrasse 42
EventPriceFact 45 chf
EventSeatsFact 20 seats

Every fact is flex items-center gap-2 with a size-4 icon at text-foreground/50. Location and price carry an sr-only label ("location", "price") because their icon is the only thing naming them; seats does not, since "20 seats" reads on its own. Colour comes from the container (text-foreground/80), not the fact.

Public formats are lowercase, admin formats keep sentence case — the two voices of the app, expressed in the formatter rather than in CSS. Every rendered date is a <time dateTime={iso}> with suppressHydrationWarning, because the server and the visitor's browser may be in different zones.
